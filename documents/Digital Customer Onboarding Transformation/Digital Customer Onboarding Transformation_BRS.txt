1. BUSINESS CONTEXT
------------------------------------------------------------

The organization currently operates a customer onboarding process that
requires multiple verification, documentation and operational steps.
The initiative is intended to transform this journey into a predominantly
digital and automated process while maintaining regulatory compliance
and appropriate control mechanisms.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Customer onboarding involves multiple manual interventions, fragmented
verification steps and dependency on external KYC services. These
dependencies increase turnaround time, operational effort and the
possibility of processing errors. Customers may also experience delays
when submitting documentation or completing verification activities.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Manual intervention across multiple onboarding stages
- Long customer turnaround time
- Dependency on multiple verification services
- Operational effort for document validation
- Limited real-time visibility into onboarding exceptions

------------------------------------------------------------
4. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

The primary objective of this initiative is to address the identified
business and operational challenges through a standardized, scalable
and controlled technology solution.

The solution should improve operational efficiency, reduce manual
intervention, improve transparency and provide measurable business
outcomes while maintaining applicable security, regulatory and
operational controls.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROPOSED SOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Implement a digitally enabled onboarding workflow incorporating digital
KYC, automated document verification, API-based integrations and
centralized operational monitoring.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. HIGH-LEVEL SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------

The scope includes:

- Business requirement analysis and process assessment
- Target-state solution design
- Application/API/data integration as required
- Development and configuration
