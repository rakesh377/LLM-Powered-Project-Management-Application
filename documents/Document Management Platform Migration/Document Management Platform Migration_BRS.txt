PROJECT: Document Management Platform Migration
======================================================================
1. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Enterprise applications generate and consume a large volume of
business documents including customer records, agreements, forms and
operational documentation. A reliable document management platform is
required to provide secure storage and controlled retrieval.


2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Legacy document management infrastructure may have scalability,
performance, integration and maintenance limitations. Migration to a
modern platform is required while preserving document integrity and
access controls.


3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS

- Legacy platform limitations
- High maintenance effort
- Limited scalability
- Difficult integration with modern applications
- Document retrieval challenges
- Migration and data integrity risks


4. OBJECTIVE

Migrate enterprise documents to a modern, scalable and secure document
management platform with minimal business disruption.


5. PROPOSED SOLUTION

Implement a controlled document migration program covering document
inventory, metadata mapping, data migration, validation, integration
and controlled decommissioning of the legacy platform.


6. HIGH-LEVEL SCOPE

- Document inventory
- Metadata assessment
- Target platform design
- Migration mapping
- Data migration
- Integrity validation
- Application integration
- User acceptance testing
- Legacy platform decommissioning


7. SYSTEMS / APPLICATIONS INVOLVED

- Legacy Document Management System
- Target Document Management Platform
- Customer Applications
- Workflow Systems
- Identity and Access Management


8. HIGH-LEVEL REQUIREMENTS

- Documents shall be migrated without loss or corruption.
- Metadata shall be mapped correctly.
- Existing access controls shall be preserved.
- Document retrieval shall meet agreed performance requirements.
- Migration shall be auditable.


9. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Agreed document population is migrated.
- Migration reconciliation is completed.
- Document integrity is validated.
- Access controls are verified.
- Business users complete UAT successfully.


10. EXPECTED BUSINESS OUTCOMES

- Reduced legacy platform dependency
- Improved scalability
- Better document retrieval
- Improved integration capability
- Reduced maintenance overhead
