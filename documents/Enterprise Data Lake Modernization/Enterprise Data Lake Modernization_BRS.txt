1. BUSINESS CONTEXT
------------------------------------------------------------

The organization has multiple enterprise systems generating large
volumes of structured and semi-structured data. The initiative aims to
modernize the existing data architecture and establish a scalable,
governed and cloud-enabled data platform.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Legacy data platforms and fragmented ingestion mechanisms make it
difficult to scale analytics, maintain consistent data quality and
provide timely access to enterprise information. Historical datasets
also require significant effort to migrate and reconcile.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Fragmented enterprise data sources
- Legacy ingestion architecture
- Inconsistent data quality
- Limited scalability for growing workloads
- High effort involved in historical data migration
- Limited centralized data lineage and governance

------------------------------------------------------------
4. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

The primary objective of this initiative is to address the identified
business and operational challenges through a standardized, scalable
and controlled technology solution.

The solution should improve operational efficiency, reduce manual
intervention, improve transparency and provide measurable business
outcomes while maintaining applicable security, regulatory and
operational controls.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROPOSED SOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Implement a scalable enterprise data lake architecture with standardized
data ingestion, automated quality controls, governed storage and
modernized processing capabilities.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. HIGH-LEVEL SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------

The scope includes:

- Business requirement analysis and process assessment
- Target-state solution design
- Application/API/data integration as required
- Development and configuration
- Data validation and quality checks
- Functional and integration testing
- User Acceptance Testing
- Production deployment
- Post-production monitoring and stabilization

Out of scope items will be governed through the formal change
management process.
