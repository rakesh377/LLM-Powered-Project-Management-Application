1. BUSINESS CONTEXT
------------------------------------------------------------

The organization processes a large volume of financial transactions
requiring continuous monitoring for potentially fraudulent activity.
Traditional rule-based approaches may not adequately identify evolving
fraud patterns.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Existing fraud detection mechanisms may generate false positives while
also being less effective against emerging transaction patterns.
Manual investigation and delayed identification can increase operational
cost and potential financial exposure.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Evolving fraud patterns
- High number of false-positive alerts
- Manual fraud investigation effort
- Limited use of behavioral transaction signals
- Need for low-latency transaction scoring

------------------------------------------------------------
4. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

The primary objective of this initiative is to address the identified
business and operational challenges through a standardized, scalable
and controlled technology solution.

The solution should improve operational efficiency, reduce manual
intervention, improve transparency and provide measurable business
outcomes while maintaining applicable security, regulatory and
operational controls.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROPOSED SOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Enhance fraud detection using machine learning models, behavioral
transaction signals and a real-time scoring API integrated with the
existing transaction processing ecosystem.

------------------------------------------------------------
