1. BUSINESS CONTEXT
------------------------------------------------------------

The organization is required to produce multiple regulatory reports
using information sourced from different enterprise systems. Several
reporting activities currently involve manual data preparation,
validation and consolidation.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Manual regulatory reporting creates operational dependency, increases
the possibility of data and calculation errors, and requires significant
effort from reporting teams. Lack of standardized lineage also makes
report validation and audit traceability more difficult.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Manual report preparation
- Multiple source systems
- High operational effort
- Potential data quality and calculation errors
- Limited automated validation
- Difficulty maintaining complete data lineage

------------------------------------------------------------
4. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

The primary objective of this initiative is to address the identified
business and operational challenges through a standardized, scalable
and controlled technology solution.

The solution should improve operational efficiency, reduce manual
intervention, improve transparency and provide measurable business
outcomes while maintaining applicable security, regulatory and
operational controls.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROPOSED SOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Automate regulatory data extraction, validation, transformation,
report generation and approval workflows while maintaining complete
auditability and source-to-report traceability.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. HIGH-LEVEL SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------

The scope includes:

- Business requirement analysis and process assessment
- Target-state solution design
- Application/API/data integration as required
- Development and configuration
- Data validation and quality checks
- Functional and integration testing
- User Acceptance Testing
- Production deployment
- Post-production monitoring and stabilization

Out of scope items will be governed through the formal change
management process.
