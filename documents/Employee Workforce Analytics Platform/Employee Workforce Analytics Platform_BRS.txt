PROJECT: Employee Workforce Analytics Platform
======================================================================
1. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Organizations generate significant workforce data through HR,
attendance, performance, recruitment and employee engagement systems.
A centralized analytics capability can improve workforce planning and
management decision-making.


2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Workforce information may be distributed across multiple systems,
making it difficult for management to obtain timely and consistent
insights.


3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS

- Fragmented workforce data
- Manual reporting
- Delayed management insights
- Limited workforce trend analysis
- Difficult cross-functional reporting


4. OBJECTIVE

Create a centralized workforce analytics platform providing reliable
and timely insights into employee-related metrics.


5. PROPOSED SOLUTION

Integrate HR and workforce systems into a governed analytics platform
with standardized KPIs, dashboards and analytical capabilities.


6. HIGH-LEVEL SCOPE

- HR data integration
- Workforce data model
- KPI standardization
- Data quality checks
- Analytics dashboards
- Workforce trend analysis
- Access controls


7. SYSTEMS / APPLICATIONS INVOLVED

- HR Management System
- Payroll System
- Recruitment Platform
- Attendance System
- Data Warehouse
- Business Intelligence Platform


8. HIGH-LEVEL REQUIREMENTS

- Standard workforce KPIs shall be defined.
- Data shall be refreshed according to agreed frequency.
- Access shall be role-based.
- Historical trends shall be retained.
- Reports shall provide consistent calculations.


9. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Required HR sources are integrated.
- KPI definitions are approved.
- Dashboards are validated.
- Data reconciliation is completed.
- Business users approve the analytical outputs.


10. EXPECTED BUSINESS OUTCOMES

- Faster workforce reporting
- Improved management decision-making
- Reduced manual reporting
- Better workforce planning
- Improved data transparency
