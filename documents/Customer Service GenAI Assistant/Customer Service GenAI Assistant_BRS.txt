1. BUSINESS CONTEXT
------------------------------------------------------------

Customer service teams handle a high volume of customer interactions
and require access to product, process and policy information while
responding to queries. The initiative aims to provide AI-assisted
information retrieval and response support to service agents.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Service agents spend considerable time searching across knowledge
repositories and interpreting internal documentation. This can increase
average handling time and create inconsistencies in responses.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS
------------------------------------------------------------

- Time spent searching knowledge repositories
- High average handling time
- Inconsistent responses across service agents
- Difficulty locating the latest policy information
- Manual escalation of complex queries

------------------------------------------------------------
4. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

The primary objective of this initiative is to address the identified
business and operational challenges through a standardized, scalable
and controlled technology solution.

The solution should improve operational efficiency, reduce manual
intervention, improve transparency and provide measurable business
outcomes while maintaining applicable security, regulatory and
operational controls.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROPOSED SOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Implement a secure GenAI assistant using retrieval-augmented generation
to provide contextual information to service agents while enforcing
appropriate response guardrails, access controls and human escalation.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. HIGH-LEVEL SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------

The scope includes:

- Business requirement analysis and process assessment
- Target-state solution design
- Application/API/data integration as required
- Development and configuration
- Data validation and quality checks
- Functional and integration testing
- User Acceptance Testing
- Production deployment
- Post-production monitoring and stabilization

Out of scope items will be governed through the formal change
management process.

14. KEY RISKS AND MITIGATION
------------------------------------------------------------

- AI hallucination (Severity: High, Probability: Medium)
  Mitigation: Implement retrieval thresholds, citations and human escalation.

  Mitigation: Implement PII masking, access controls and prompt filtering.

  Mitigation: Run pilot programs and provide targeted training.
