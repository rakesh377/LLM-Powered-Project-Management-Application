PROJECT: Enterprise Customer Data 360 Platform
======================================================================
1. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Customer information is distributed across multiple products,
applications and operational systems. A unified customer view is
required to support analytics, service operations and personalized
customer engagement.


2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Fragmented customer information makes it difficult for business and
service teams to obtain a complete and consistent view of a customer.


3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS

- Duplicate customer records
- Fragmented customer information
- Inconsistent data across systems
- Limited customer-level analytics
- Manual reconciliation
- Difficulty delivering personalized experiences


4. OBJECTIVE

Create a unified customer data platform that provides a trusted,
consistent and governed view of customer information.


5. PROPOSED SOLUTION

Implement a Customer 360 platform that consolidates customer data from
multiple enterprise sources, applies data quality and identity
resolution rules, and exposes governed customer information to
authorized consumers.


6. HIGH-LEVEL SCOPE

- Customer data ingestion
- Data standardization
- Customer identity resolution
- Duplicate detection
- Data quality management
- Customer 360 data model
- API/data access layer
- Governance and lineage


7. SYSTEMS / APPLICATIONS INVOLVED

- Core Banking Systems
- CRM
- Loan Systems
- Credit Card Systems
- Digital Banking
- Enterprise Data Platform


8. HIGH-LEVEL REQUIREMENTS

- Customer records shall be uniquely identifiable.
- Duplicate customer records shall be detected.
- Data quality rules shall be implemented.
- Source-to-target lineage shall be maintained.
- Access shall follow data security policies.


9. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Agreed source systems are successfully integrated.
- Customer matching accuracy meets the agreed threshold.
- Data quality rules operate successfully.
- Customer 360 records are accessible to authorized consumers.
- Data governance sign-off is completed.


10. EXPECTED BUSINESS OUTCOMES

- Improved customer understanding
- Better analytics
- Reduced data duplication
- Improved service experience
- Better personalization capabilities
