PROJECT DESCRIPTION
======================================================================

Modernization of enterprise identity management and privileged access controls.


======================================================================
1. BUSINESS CONTEXT
======================================================================


1. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Enterprise applications require secure and controlled access to
protect sensitive business and customer information. As application
landscapes evolve, identity and access management capabilities must
also evolve.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Legacy authentication mechanisms and fragmented access-management
processes can increase security risk and operational effort.

3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS

- Fragmented identity management
- Manual access provisioning
- Delayed access revocation
- Inconsistent authorization controls
- Limited centralized visibility

4. OBJECTIVE

Modernize identity and access management to provide secure,
centralized and auditable access across enterprise applications.

5. PROPOSED SOLUTION

Implement centralized authentication, role-based authorization,
automated provisioning/deprovisioning and access governance.

6. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Critical applications are integrated.
- Role-based access controls are implemented.
- Provisioning and deprovisioning workflows are validated.
- Access audit trails are available.
- Security testing is completed.

7. EXPECTED BUSINESS OUTCOMES

- Improved security
- Reduced access-management effort
- Faster employee onboarding
- Improved compliance
- Better auditability
