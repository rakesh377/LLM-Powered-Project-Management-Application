PROJECT: Payment Platform Reliability Enhancement
======================================================================
1. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Payment processing is a mission-critical capability requiring high
availability, low latency and strong transaction integrity. Any
significant disruption can directly impact customers and business
operations.


2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Payment failures, service interruptions and delayed transaction
processing can negatively impact customer trust and create operational
and financial reconciliation challenges.


3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS

- Payment transaction failures
- Service interruptions
- Delayed transaction responses
- Reconciliation exceptions
- Limited incident visibility
- Manual operational intervention


4. OBJECTIVE

Improve payment platform reliability, availability and transaction
processing resilience.


5. PROPOSED SOLUTION

Enhance the payment platform through improved monitoring, automated
failure handling, retry mechanisms, resilient service architecture and
stronger transaction reconciliation controls.


6. HIGH-LEVEL SCOPE

- Payment service assessment
- Failure analysis
- Retry mechanism
- Transaction monitoring
- Reconciliation enhancement
- Resilience testing
- Incident monitoring
- Disaster recovery validation


7. SYSTEMS / APPLICATIONS INVOLVED

- Payment Processing Platform
- Core Banking System
- API Gateway
- Payment Networks
- Transaction Database
- Monitoring Platform


8. HIGH-LEVEL REQUIREMENTS

- Payment transactions shall maintain end-to-end traceability.
- Failed transactions shall be appropriately handled.
- Retry mechanisms shall prevent duplicate transactions.
- Reconciliation exceptions shall be identifiable.
- Critical payment services shall meet availability SLAs.


9. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Agreed availability target is achieved.
- Failure scenarios are successfully tested.
- Reconciliation process is validated.
- Monitoring and alerting are operational.
- No critical transaction integrity issues remain.


10. EXPECTED BUSINESS OUTCOMES

- Improved payment reliability
- Reduced transaction failures
- Reduced operational intervention
- Improved customer trust
- Better transaction monitoring
