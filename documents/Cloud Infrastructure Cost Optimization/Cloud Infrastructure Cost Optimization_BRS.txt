PROJECT: Cloud Infrastructure Cost Optimization
======================================================================
1. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Increasing adoption of cloud infrastructure has resulted in growing
compute, storage, networking and managed-service expenditure. Effective
cloud cost governance is required to ensure that infrastructure spend
remains aligned with business usage.


2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Cloud resources may be underutilized or provisioned above actual
requirements. Lack of centralized cost visibility and automated
optimization can result in unnecessary infrastructure expenditure.


3. BUSINESS PAIN POINTS

- Underutilized compute resources
- Unnecessary storage consumption
- Idle cloud resources
- Lack of cost ownership
- Limited forecasting accuracy
- Manual cost optimization activities


4. OBJECTIVE

Reduce unnecessary cloud expenditure while maintaining required
performance, availability and security standards.


5. PROPOSED SOLUTION

Implement cloud cost monitoring, resource utilization analysis,
rightsizing, automated lifecycle policies, reserved-capacity
optimization and business-unit level cost allocation.


6. HIGH-LEVEL SCOPE

- Cloud resource inventory
- Utilization analysis
- Rightsizing recommendations
- Storage optimization
- Idle resource identification
- Cost dashboards
- Budget alerts
- Automated cleanup policies


7. SYSTEMS / APPLICATIONS INVOLVED

- Cloud Infrastructure
- Compute Services
- Storage Services
- Database Services
- Monitoring Platform
- Cost Management Platform


8. HIGH-LEVEL REQUIREMENTS

- Cloud expenditure shall be visible by application or business owner.
- Underutilized resources shall be identified.
- Budget thresholds shall generate alerts.
- Optimization activities shall not compromise production SLAs.
- Cost savings shall be measurable.


9. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

- Target cost reduction is achieved.
- Cost dashboards are available.
- Idle resources are identified and addressed.
- Budget alerts are operational.
- No material production performance degradation occurs.


10. EXPECTED BUSINESS OUTCOMES

- Reduced cloud expenditure
- Improved infrastructure utilization
- Better financial governance
- Improved cost forecasting
- Increased accountability for technology spend
